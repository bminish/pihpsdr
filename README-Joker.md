# piHPSDR Joker

The urgent need for new standards has left us with no sensible choice but to resurrect an old one.

We are confident that, once the wisdom and beauty of representing one's place in the RF spectrum using
human-designed units is experienced, this will rapidly become the new standard by which we navigate the
crowded airwaves.

## The dial

VFO A and VFO B read wavelength in feet, inches and fractions of an inch, to the nearest 1⁄144″, in a typeface
fit for a Victorian drawing office. RIT and XIT read in mils (1⁄1000″) of wavelength: moving up in frequency
shortens the wave, so a positive offset in Hz reads as negative mils.

A legacy display mode in MHz may be selected in the Theme menu (**VFO Dial**: *Frequency*). A *Metres* setting,
in base-10 decimals with RIT and XIT in millimetres, is provided for those who must.

The RADE Reporter shows stations by the wavelength at the centre of their signal, in the same units as the dial,
and announces this station to the FreeDV Reporter as `pihpsdr-Imperial-edition`.

The typeface is Latin Modern Roman Dunhill. If it is not installed, Old Standard, Century Schoolbook or the
system serif is used instead.

## Fineness of the division

One division of 1⁄144″ is about 8 Hz on 80 m, 29 Hz on 40 m, 120 Hz on 20 m, 475 Hz on 10 m and 1.5 kHz on 6 m.
Tuning between divisions still works; the dial simply declines to comment.
